# Care visit recording — 0.5

Reviewed 13 November 2026. These fields support ASHA recordkeeping and counselling tracking; they do not prescribe treatment or replace the official MCP card.

## Pregnancy and postnatal visits

The mother-and-child protection guide describes pregnancy registration, the MCP card, ANC records, nutrition counselling, injections, delivery preparation and postnatal support. DARA records reported months/weeks, card status, foods/supplements, injection details/count, care facility and last/next visit. A postnatal stage and delivery date retain the same person’s care context. Card access is not a prerequisite for recording a visit.

A short checklist tracks discussion of the card, injections, nutrition, ANC, birth planning and postnatal care. Extraction must cite an actual question or counselling exchange. A patient saying “one injection taken” records history; it does not prove injection counselling occurred. Unknown injection type or date stays unknown. Months are not converted to invented exact weeks.

Sources:

- [NHM MCP Guidebook for ANM, ASHA and AWW](https://www.nhm.gov.in/New_Updates_2018/NHM_Components/Immunization/Guildelines_for_immunization/MCP_Guide_Book.pdf), registration, ANC, delivery preparation and postnatal sections.
- [NHM Maternal Health Guidance for Community Health Officers](https://www.nhm.gov.in/New_Update-2022-23/MH/GUIDELINES-%20MH/CHO_Booklet_%20Maternal_Health-English.pdf), antenatal services and community follow-up.

## Rainy-season visits

Known family identities can be reused for a new visit, but prior health answers and completed checklist items are not silently carried forward. Ask about fever/cold or other concerns per person, and record stagnant water and its location. Checklist topics cover symptom enquiry, emptying/cleaning containers and pots, mosquito protection and dengue/malaria awareness, and when/where to seek care.

The official vector-control programme describes ASHA fever surveys, covered storage, weekly emptying and scrubbing of containers, and removal of discarded water-holding items. This informs the enquiry and counselling topics, not automated diagnosis. The app does not infer dengue or malaria from fever or environmental answers.

Source: [NHM Maharashtra — National Vector Borne Disease Control Programme](https://nhm.maharashtra.gov.in/en/scheme/national-vector-born-disease-control-programme-nvbdcp/), surveillance and dengue/chikungunya prevention sections.

## Recording boundaries

- Silence means not discussed, never healthy by default.
- A numerical temperature without its unit remains a reported value requiring clarification; it is not silently assigned Celsius.
- Manual edits stay protected from background backfill.
- Fictional example injections are sample history, not a recommended schedule.
- Actual field accuracy and official questionnaire approval remain separate validation work.

## Follow-up behavior

Pregnancy follow-up is offered from confirmed pregnancy/postnatal history. Carried care details show their prior source and require review; health observations and counselling checklists start fresh. Household members must be confirmed for each repeat visit. A confirmed pregnant member receives an editable date 14 days after the visit date; this is an editable recordkeeping suggestion, not an individual clinical recommendation. A worker-entered or deliberately cleared date is preserved.
